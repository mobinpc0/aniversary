To Liza

Three years ago I didn't know what our ordinary days would look like — I just knew I wanted to find out with you. Now I know: they look like boba runs, voice notes at odd hours, and someone always asking "amake bhalobashen?" and getting the same answer, every time.

Thank you for staying, for arguing with me only when it mattered, and for making a random Tuesday feel like an occasion just by being in it. You have made the last three years the easiest hard years of my life.

Here's to the version of us that's still being written.
